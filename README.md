# FYP-PROJECT
In recent times, 3D printing has become the go to method of prototyping 
among engineers but this method of prototyping has added to the persistent 
problem of plastic waste. This plastic waste from 3D printed prototyping can be 
negated by recycling wasted 3d printing filament and making it a new. The goal of 
the project is to provide a better alternative to 3D printing companies than to import 
expensive 3D printing filament and waste residual filament left behind by printing. 
This 3D Printer Filament Extruder Mechanism would provide a low cost and 
recyclable method to make 3d printing more lucrative and inexpensive. This 
Project will describe the design process, including the selection of materials, the 
choice of components, and the assembly of the extruder. This project will 
demonstrate the feasibility and effectiveness of producing 3D printer filament 
using a custom-built extruder. The extruder has the potential to significantly reduce 
the cost of 3D printing by producing high-quality filament at a lower cost than 
commercially available filaments.
